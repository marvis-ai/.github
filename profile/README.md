## Open Source Projects

### 🖥️ [marvis](https://github.com/marvis-ai/marvis)

**A privacy-first AI co-pilot that lives on your desktop.**

An always-available assistant that floats above your workspace as a small
translucent bar. It can see your screen (with permission), hear your meetings,
and answer questions about what's on screen — while your API keys, history, and
screen data stay entirely on your machine.

- **Screen-aware Ask** — grabs your latest screen frame and streams answers from
  the LLM of your choice
- **Listen** — mic + system-audio meeting capture with live transcription and
  rolling summaries
- **Bring your own model** — OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, OpenRouter, local
  [Ollama](https://ollama.com), or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, with a
  drag-to-reorder failover chain
- **Local-first** — screenshots never touch disk; sessions live in SQLite under
  `~/.marvis`. No accounts, no cloud sync

`Tauri 2` · `Rust` · `React` · macOS · Windows · Linux ·
[MIT](https://github.com/marvis-ai/marvis/blob/master/LICENSE)

### 📰 [enhancer](https://github.com/marvis-ai/enhancer)

**A browser extension that gives news sites a clean, modern reading experience.**

Starting with Hacker News, Enhancer AI replaces cluttered pages with a beautiful
React-rendered interface — without breaking what makes the site work.

- **Modern UI** — improved typography, spacing, and visual hierarchy
- **Dark mode** — follows your system preference automatically
- **Reading tools** — scroll-progress bar, scroll-to-top, domain badges, enhanced
  comment threads
- **Customizable** — font sizes, compact mode, and per-site toggles

`TypeScript` · `React 19` · `Tailwind 4` · `Vite` · Manifest V3 ·
Chrome · Edge · Brave · MIT
